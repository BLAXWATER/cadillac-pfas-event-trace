ROAD COMMISSION / WWTP / 1121 PLETT ROAD — ORIGINAL RECORDS INDEX
Compiled: 2026-09-01
Project searched: MDOT / Wexford County Road Commission contract 83609-108868

BOTTOM LINE

No publicly posted original located in this search expressly states that Wexford County
Road Commission personnel coordinated with, directed, or received direction from Cadillac
WWTP personnel during the 2012 bridge/box-culvert work.

The strongest contemporaneous record is the City Council packet dated August 6, 2012. In
the same packet and meeting, the Council (1) considered and approved the Wexford County
Road Commission agreement for the Plett Road / 13th Street project and (2) separately
approved a replacement return-activated-sludge pump for the WWTP. The record treats these
as separate agenda matters and does not link them. The original packet is included.

The bridge contract records do document storm-sewer, pipe-video, drainage, underdrain,
foundation-excavation, temporary-sheet-piling, and box-culvert work. MDOT's contract
description calls the sewer component "storm sewer." These line items alone are not proof
that the contractor worked on a sanitary sewer or interacted with WWTP staff.

The Road Commission agreement says the Road Commission designed and would construct the
project according to plans and specifications "on file with the City," and makes those plans
an integral part of the agreement. Those plans, specifications, as-builts, inspection logs,
daily reports, utility-conflict records, and 2012 Road Commission minutes were not found in
the agencies' public web archives. They are the best next records to request directly.

03_GEOLOGY_GROUNDWATER_AND_FLOW

USGS_SIR_2004-5175_Cadillac_Groundwater_Flow.pdf
  Original USGS Scientific Investigations Report 2004-5175, prepared with the City of
  Cadillac. It contains:
    - surficial glacial-deposit mapping;
    - a hydrogeologic cross section;
    - potentiometric-surface maps;
    - a conceptual groundwater-flow diagram;
    - recharge mapping;
    - modeled groundwater heads; and
    - 10-year contributing-area maps for the municipal well field.
  It reports regional deep groundwater flow from southeast to northwest and describes thick
  Pleistocene glacial deposits, sand-and-gravel aquifers, clay confining units, and Clam
  River watershed hydrology.
  Official landing page: https://www.usgs.gov/publications/simulation-ground-water-flow-and-areas-contributing-ground-water-production-wells
  Original official file URL: http://pubs.usgs.gov/sir/2004/5175/MIsir2004-5175_report.pdf
  Retrieval note: the present USGS file server blocked automated retrieval. This is the

LOCATION RELATIONSHIPS (APPROXIMATE)

The Census address match for 1121 Plett is an interpolated street address, not a surveyed
parcel corner. From that point, the EGLE WWTP point is about 289 m / 947 ft away, the MDOT
bridge point about 428 m / 1,404 ft away, and the USGS Plett Road stream-measurement point
about 420 m / 1,377 ft away. The MDOT bridge and USGS stream points are about 17 m apart.
