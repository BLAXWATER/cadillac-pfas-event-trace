Laboratory

The Cadillac Laboratory performs the following services:

  • Performing microbiological and chemical testing of the City’s distribution system
  • Analyzing the wastewater treatment plant’s influent and effluent for permit requirements
  • Conducting microbiological testing for area municipalities, contractors, and citizens for drinking and surface water

The U.S. EPA Safe Drinking Water Act requires that public water supply samples be tested by a certified laboratory. In Michigan, laboratory accreditations are performed by State of Michigan staff.

The Cadillac Laboratory is a Michigan EGLE-Certified Lab for Drinking Water.
